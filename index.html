How it works

1

Sensors go in the soil and the sky

Waterproof ground sensors measure moisture and temperature, while a weather sensor tracks rainfall, humidity, and short-term forecasts — no wiring, runs on battery for months.

2

Readings reach your phone

Data travels over a long-range signal built for farms with no WiFi. You get a simple message — no charts to decode.

3

You act, or it acts for you

Get an alert and irrigate yourself, or switch on automatic watering — the system checks the weather forecast first, so it won't water right before rain.

Built around what actually matters on a farm

Works without reliable internet

Plain-language alerts

No dashboards to learn. Messages tell you what to do, not just numbers to interpret.

You stay in control

Weather-aware watering

Local rain and heat forecasts are factored in automatically, so you never irrigate right before a downpour.
